Identity system 01

A personal AI assistant that runs on your computer.

Compa is a friendly, capable pal for everyday work. A complete visual system for a web app on your computer and a Go runtime developers can embed.

Compa's C, holding your core.

The open C holds a distinct core without closing around it: your stuff stays with you, on your computer. The system is friendly without being cute, and capable without implying autonomy.
